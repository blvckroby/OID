01 About OID

How our inspectors describe themselves

OID is a quality control and product development company based in Foshan, in the manufacturing heart of Guangdong. For more than twenty years our people have worked on the production floor on behalf of international companies.

QA / QC What we do

We work on the product, where quality is decided.

We make sure every product meets the highest standards of quality, safety and compliance — through targeted inspections, analysis of production processes, and checks on materials and finished products.

Quality Control is fundamental in every production line. It is part of a broader Quality Management system, where Quality Assurance monitors and manages production processes to ensure compliance with regulations and corporate requirements. The QC department intervenes directly on the product, performing continuous checks and tests for constant reliability, safety and performance.

The goal: prevent defects, reduce waste and improve customer satisfaction — with reliable, consistent results.

Service 02

Factory audit & supplier verification

Modern companies, often reliant on external sourcing, must ensure that their manufacturing partners are reliable, certified and compliant with required specifications. OID offers supplier verification services throughout China, ensuring compliance with international quality and safety standards.

Verification objectives

  • Assess the supplier’s production capacity, quality and legal compliance.
  • Ensure consistent, high-level supplies.
  • Reduce operational and non-compliance risks.

When to perform it

At the start of new projects or supply relationships, in order to:

  • Qualify new suppliers.
  • Verify existing partners.
  • Ensure continuity and transparency in the supply chain.

Advantages

  • Control over reliability before contracts are signed.
  • Qualification of suppliers aligned with company standards.
  • Data and images verified by an independent body.

With a presence primarily in Guangdong, OID delivers professional, impartial and accurate audits, supporting quality supplies and solid partnerships over time.

Service 03

Initial production inspection (IPI)

As production begins, before the bulk of the order is manufactured.

Objectives

  • Reduce operational and non-compliance risks for the rest of the order.

Deliverable

Service 04

During production inspection (DPI)

Also known as In-Process Inspection, it is performed when production reaches 10–20% of the cycle, in order to identify early defects and ensure compliance with technical and quality specifications. OID carries it out in China with local teams of highly qualified inspectors.

Control levels

  • Level 1100%of products
  • Level 250%of products
  • Level 325%of products

Objectives

  • Verify compliance with quality standards, timelines and contractual requirements.
  • Allow immediate corrections in case of non-compliance.
  • Ensure the consistency of the production process.

Verification activities

  • Visual, dimensional and functional checks.
  • Specific tests: drop, adhesion, resistance, barcodes, etc.
  • Verification of packaging and labelling.

Advantages

  • Early detection of defects and reduced rejection risk.
  • Greater control over batch quality and delivery times.
  • Detailed reports and inspection certificates (upon request).
  • Intervention within 48 hours; reports with detailed photos and videos.

A fast, flexible service that complies with international standards — for transparency, reliability and production continuity.

Service 06

Pre-shipment inspection (PSI)

Carried out when products are 100% complete and approximately 70% packaged, to verify quantity, quality, performance, packaging and compliance with legal or contractual standards. Independent and impartial checks on products ready for export.

Main objectives

  • Ensure compliance with technical, contractual and regulatory requirements.
  • Validate the quality declared by suppliers through third-party audit.
  • Support commercial transparency and trust.

Inspection scope

  • Functional, dimensional and durability checks.
  • Verification of packaging, labelling and markings/trademarks.
  • Application of statistical sampling procedures.
  • Final shipment compliance report, also usable for customs procedures.

Advantages

  • Reduces risks of returns or disputes.
  • Improves transparency throughout the global supply chain.
  • Credible documentation for customers and authorities.

Every inspection is customised to the client’s and the product’s needs, for maximum reliability, accuracy and compliance with international standards.
